How to Play

Rites of Passage is a text-based multiplayer RPG (MUD). You type commands to interact with the world, other players, and monsters. This guide covers the essentials so you can jump in and start your adventure.

Getting Started

Connecting to the Game

You can play directly in your browser using the Web Client, or connect via a dedicated MUD client (Telnet) to your-server.com on port 4010.

Creating an Account

When you connect, you'll see the login screen. Type:

create

Pick a strong password. Your account persists between sessions.

Logging In

Already have an account? Type:

connect

Character Creation

After logging in, you'll enter the character creation menu where you can:

  • Create a new character — pick your race, class, and name.
  • Select an existing character — if you've played before.
  • Delete characters — to start fresh.

Basic Commands

Every action in Rites of Passage is driven by typed commands. Here are the essentials:

CommandWhat It Does
lookDescribe the room you're in, including players, objects, and exits.
n, s, e, wMove north, south, east, or west.
ne, nw, se, swDiagonal movement.
in / outEnter or leave a building or portal.
say Speak aloud to everyone in the room.
tell Send a private message to another player.
helpShow the help menu. help for specifics.
inventory or iList what you're carrying.
get Pick up an item from the ground.
drop Drop an item from your inventory.
examine Inspect an object, player, or monster closely.

Combat

Starting a Fight

attack

Initiates combat with the named creature or player. You'll automatically continue attacking each round until one side is defeated or you flee.

Combat Options

CommandEffect
attack Begin basic physical attacks.
cast Cast a spell at the target (requires learned spells & mana).
fleeAttempt to escape from combat (may fail).
statsView your current health, mana, and attributes.

Death

When your HP reaches zero, you die. You'll respawn at your faction's home base (Aethelgard for Good, Gorgoroth for Evil). You may lose some experience or items upon death, so be careful!

Advanced Tips

Aliases

Most commands have short aliases. For example, n for north, i for inventory, l for look, sc for score (stats). Learn these to speed up your play.

Exploring

The world is massive. Don't be afraid to wander — but keep an eye on your health and the level of monsters in the area. consider gives you an idea of how tough a fight will be.

Quests & NPCs

Talk to NPCs: say hello in a room with an NPC may trigger dialogue or quest hints. Interact with objects: pull lever, open chest.

Grouping

Party up with other players to tackle harder content. Parties share experience. Use follow to stick together.
