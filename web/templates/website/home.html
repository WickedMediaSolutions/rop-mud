The World Awaits

Rites of Passage is a text-based multiplayer RPG (MUD) set in a sprawling dark fantasy world. Two factions stand locked in eternal conflict. With 16 unique races and 10 distinct classes, your path is yours to forge.

Will you be a noble High Elf Paladin smiting darkness with holy fire? A cunning Goblin Rogue stalking shadowed corridors? A Minotaur Warrior whose footsteps shake the ground? A Pixie Mage weaving illusion and flame?
