Key Locations

☀️

Aethelgard

The Grand Sanctum — starting city for all Good-aligned characters. High walls, holy light, and ancient wisdom protect this bastion of hope.

💀

Gorgoroth

The Blood Forge — starting citadel for Evil-aligned characters. Molten rivers, obsidian spires, and the endless drums of war echo through these halls.

🌲

The Wilds

Between the faction cities lies untamed wilderness — dark forests, crumbling ruins, monster lairs, and ancient dungeons filled with peril and plunder.

🏰

Dungeons

Scattered across the realm are instanced dungeons for parties of all levels. Brave the depths to earn legendary loot and eternal glory.
